# What is prosopography?

Prosopography is the study of groups of people through the collective examination of
their lives, often using historical records, literary sources, and artifacts.<sup><a id="fnref1" href="#fn1"><small>1</small></a></sup>

Rather than focusing on a single individual, prosopography looks for patterns across a
group, i.e., shared roles, relationships, or social status, to understand historical
societies and social structures.
